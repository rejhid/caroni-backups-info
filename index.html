Caroni Backups copies encrypted backups of a self-hosted budgeting app to the owner’s Google Drive. It is maintained for personal household use.

Read the privacy policy →

01

Encrypt locally

The database dump and app files are encrypted with restic before upload. The decryption password stays outside Google Drive.

02

Copy to Drive

Rclone uploads completed backup files to the owner’s designated folder over verified HTTPS.

03

Verify and recover

The workflow verifies uploaded backup data and supports restoring it to an isolated replacement database.

About Google authorization

The integration requests permission to manage Drive files. Google grants access across your Drive, rather than restricting the grant to the backup folder. The configured backup workflow uses the designated backup folder and its retention archive.

Authorization is completed in your browser. This page does not collect passwords, request a Google login, or connect to your Drive.
